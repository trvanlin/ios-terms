Trip Tamer for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Trip Tamer is built so that your trips stay yours. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours holding your data. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

The short version

  • Your trips are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We can't see them.
  • We don't collect analytics, show ads, track you, or include third-party tracking code.
  • Your information leaves your phone only when you do something that needs it to: open a map, search for a place, open a company's website, share a trip, or ask Trip Tamer to read a confirmation.

Your trips

Trips and everything in them (flights, hotels, rental cars, plans, notes, confirmation numbers) are saved on your device and synced through your private iCloud database, using Apple's iCloud. Only you can get to them, from devices signed in to your Apple ID. If you're not signed in to iCloud, trips stay on your device only.

Deleting a trip deletes it from your device and your iCloud, so it's gone from all your devices. Deleting the app only removes the copy on that phone; to remove your trips from iCloud too, delete them in the app first.

Your settings (appearance, theme, accent color) are stored on your device only.

Built-in lists

The airport list, time zones, airlines, aircraft, rental companies and company websites are built into the app. Looking things up in them (filling in a city, working out miles or flight times, suggesting names) happens on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

When information leaves your phone

Each of these happens only when you use the feature.

Maps. When you open an address, it's handed to the maps app you choose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze), as if you'd typed it there. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Place search. When you type a hotel, restaurant, activity or rental car location, the text you type is sent to Apple's map service to suggest places, the same as searching in the Maps app. Searches are centered on your trip's destination airport. When a confirmation doesn't include a hotel's, restaurant's or activity's address, Trip Tamer looks up its name near your trip's destination the same way and shows you what it found to check before you add it. Trip Tamer never asks for your location. Apple's privacy policy covers these searches.

Reading a pasted confirmation. When you paste a booking email or confirmation and tap Import, that text is sent over an encrypted connection, through a small relay we run on Cloudflare, to Anthropic's Claude, which reads it and returns the trip details for you to review. If you share, choose or paste a PDF or a screenshot instead, its text is read on your phone first, shown to you, and only that text is sent. The file or picture itself never leaves your phone. Screenshots or text you share to Trip Tamer from another app wait on your phone until you open Trip Tamer, and are removed from there as soon as it opens them (or after a day). The relay doesn't store or log what you send; it keeps only a count of imports per phone for the day, under a random ID with no personal information, which is deleted after two days. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models, and keeps it only as long as its own policy allows for safety and abuse checks (see anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Nothing is saved to your trips until you review it and add it. Don't send anything you wouldn't want read this way, such as payment card numbers.

Purchases. Premium (reading more confirmations than the free tries allow, the full Stats screen, and the extra themes) can be unlocked with a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells us nothing about who bought; Trip Tamer only asks your phone whether your Apple ID owns Premium, and Family Sharing applies. Until you unlock, a small count of free tries used (0 to 10) is kept on your device so that reinstalling the app doesn't reset it. Once you unlock, your device also remembers that Premium is on, so the app opens unlocked even before the App Store answers. Neither is tied to you, and neither leaves your phone.

Stats. The Stats tab (miles, flights, hotel nights, routes, airlines, aircraft, states and countries) is worked out on your phone from the trips already on it. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you turn a state or country on or off by hand, that choice stays on your phone and isn't synced.

Opening a company. "Open [company]" opens the airline's, hotel's or rental company's home page, in their app if you have it, otherwise in Safari. Nothing about your trip is included in the link. The company's own privacy policy applies once you're on their site or app.

Sharing. "Share Trip" and "Share" on an address create text on your phone and hand it to the share sheet. It goes only where you send it. Shared trips include confirmation numbers.

Copying. "Copy" puts text, such as a confirmation number, on your clipboard only when you tap it.

Beta testing

If you're testing Trip Tamer through TestFlight, Apple may send us crash reports and any feedback you choose to submit, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. This doesn't happen in the App Store version unless you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings.

Children

Trip Tamer isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change affects how your information leaves your phone, the app will tell you before it happens.
