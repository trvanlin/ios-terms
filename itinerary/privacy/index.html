Itinerary for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Itinerary is built so that your trips stay yours. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours holding your data. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

The short version

  • Your trips are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We can't see them.
  • We don't collect analytics, show ads, track you, or include third-party tracking code.
  • Your information leaves your phone only when you do something that needs it to: open a map, search for a place, open a company's website, or share a trip.

Your trips

Trips and everything in them (flights, hotels, rental cars, plans, notes, confirmation numbers) are saved on your device and synced through your private iCloud database, using Apple's iCloud. Only you can get to them, from devices signed in to your Apple ID. If you're not signed in to iCloud, trips stay on your device only.

Deleting a trip deletes it from your device and your iCloud, so it's gone from all your devices. Deleting the app only removes the copy on that phone; to remove your trips from iCloud too, delete them in the app first.

Your settings (appearance, color theme) are stored on your device only.

Built-in lists

The airport list, time zones, airlines, aircraft, rental companies and company websites are built into the app. Looking things up in them (filling in a city, working out miles or flight times, suggesting names) happens on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

When information leaves your phone

Each of these happens only when you use the feature.

Maps. When you open an address, it's handed to the maps app you choose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze), as if you'd typed it there. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Place search. When you type a hotel, restaurant, activity or rental car location, the text you type is sent to Apple's map service to suggest places, the same as searching in the Maps app. Searches are centered on your trip's destination airport. Itinerary never asks for your location. Apple's privacy policy covers these searches.

Opening a company. "Open [company]" opens the airline's, hotel's or rental company's home page, in their app if you have it, otherwise in Safari. Nothing about your trip is included in the link. The company's own privacy policy applies once you're on their site or app.

Sharing. "Share Trip" and "Share" on an address create text on your phone and hand it to the share sheet. It goes only where you send it. Shared trips include confirmation numbers.

Copying. "Copy" puts text, such as a confirmation number, on your clipboard only when you tap it.

Beta testing

If you're testing Itinerary through TestFlight, Apple may send us crash reports and any feedback you choose to submit, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. This doesn't happen in the App Store version unless you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings.

Children

Itinerary isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change affects how your information leaves your phone, the app will tell you before it happens.
